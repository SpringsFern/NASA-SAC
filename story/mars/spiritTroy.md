## the sand trap that ended a marathon

Spirit landed in Gusev Crater in 2004, twin sister of Opportunity. It was supposed to last 90 days. It lasted years, climbing hills and surviving dust storms that should have killed it.

Then in 2009 it broke through a thin crust of soil into soft sand underneath, at a place the team named Troy. The wheels just spun. NASA spent months trying to rock it free, even building test setups on Earth to practice. Nothing worked, and the rover was tilted in a way that meant its solar panels couldn't catch enough sun to survive the coming winter.

The last message came on 22 March 2010. NASA kept calling for over a year and finally gave up on 25 May 2011. Spirit is still there at Troy, a monument to a rover that went way, way past its warranty.
