## "my battery is low and it's getting dark"

Opportunity (Oppy to her fans) landed in 2004 and just kept going. 90-day mission turned into 14 years. She drove over 45 km, found the first meteorite ever spotted on Mars (next to her own discarded heat shield, which is a fun bit of irony), and explored crater after crater.

In 2018 a huge dust storm covered the whole planet and blocked the sun. Oppy's solar panels couldn't charge. Her last signal came on 10 June 2018. A reporter translated the data into "my battery is low and it's getting dark," and the internet lost it. To be clear, the rover didn't literally say that in English, it was a poetic way to describe the readings. But it fit.

NASA sent over 1,000 wake-up calls and declared the mission over on 13 February 2019. She's still parked in Perseverance Valley, covered in dust.
