## the first wheels on Mars

In 1997, Pathfinder bounced onto Mars wrapped in airbags, opened up like a flower, and rolled out Sojourner, a rover about the size of a microwave. It was the first rover ever to drive on another planet.

Sojourner didn't go far. It mostly circled the lander, poking at rocks like "Barnacle Bill" and "Yogi." Total distance was just over 100 meters. But it proved the whole idea worked, and every bigger rover after it owes something to this little guy.

Contact was lost on 27 September 1997 for reasons nobody fully figured out. The last command sent told Sojourner to stay put and wait. So as far as we know, it's still parked near the lander, waiting for instructions that never came. The lander was later renamed the Carl Sagan Memorial Station. Both are still sitting on that ancient flood plain.
