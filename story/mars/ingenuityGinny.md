## the helicopter that wouldn't stop flying

Ingenuity (Ginny) was a tiny helicopter that rode to Mars under the Perseverance rover. It was meant to fly 5 times as a tech demo. That's it. Instead it flew 72 times over almost three years, becoming the first aircraft to fly on another planet.

On 18 January 2024, during its 72nd flight, a rotor blade got damaged on landing and that was the end of flying. The team named the final spot Valinor Hills, after the undying lands in Lord of the Rings, which is a pretty sweet tribute.

Here's the part I like: it's not totally done. NASA set it up to wake up every day, take a picture, and record weather data, like a little stationary science station. It's grounded forever but still working. Note: this pin is approximate (the Jezero landing area), since NASA hasn't published exact coordinates for Valinor Hills.
