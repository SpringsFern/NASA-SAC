## it found the ice, then the ice got it

Phoenix landed near the north pole of Mars in May 2008. Its whole job was to dig into the frozen ground, and it did exactly that. It scooped up soil, found water ice, and even watched snow falling from the clouds. Pretty amazing for a lander that only lasted about five months.

As the arctic summer ended, the sun got lower and the power dropped. The last signal came on 2 November 2008. Everyone hoped it might wake up after the winter, like a little hibernating robot. It didn't.

A year later the HiRISE camera on an orbiter took a photo and showed why: the winter ice and CO2 frost had snapped one of the solar panels. Before-and-after pictures make it obvious. Phoenix is still there, broken panel and all, frozen into the arctic it came to study.
