## the first car on another world

Apollo 15 was the first mission to bring a car. The Lunar Roving Vehicle (LRV) was this fold-up electric buggy that let the crew drive way farther than they could walk. They explored the Hadley Rille area and the mountains next to it.

When it was time to leave, they parked the rover a bit away from the lander, pointed its camera at the ascent stage, and let it record the liftoff. So the buggy's last job was filming its own ride home leaving. It's still parked in that spot, which the team nicknamed the VIP site.

The crew also left a tiny aluminum figure called Fallen Astronaut, with a plaque listing 14 American and Soviet space travelers who had died. It's a quiet little memorial sitting in the dust, and honestly it hits harder than you'd expect.
