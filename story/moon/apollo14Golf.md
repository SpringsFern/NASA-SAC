## the most famous golf shot ever

Apollo 14 landed in the Fra Mauro hills in 1971. The crew had a two-wheeled hand cart called the MET to haul their gear around. It's still parked out there somewhere.

But everyone remembers this mission for the golf. Alan Shepard smuggled a 6-iron club head and two golf balls in his suit pocket. He attached the head to a lunar sampling tool and took a swing. The suit was so stiff he could only use one hand. First shots were mostly dirt, but he caught the last one and famously said it went "miles and miles and miles." (It was probably more like 40 yards, but let him have it.)

So somewhere near this pin there are two golf balls that have been sitting in the dust for over 50 years. Probably the most littered golf course in the solar system.
