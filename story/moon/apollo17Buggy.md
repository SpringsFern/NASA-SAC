## the last ride, and a duct tape fix

Apollo 17 in 1972 was the last time anyone walked on the Moon. Gene Cernan and Harrison Schmitt drove the third and final moon buggy around the Taurus-Littrow valley.

Early on, Cernan's hammer snagged the rover's fender and snapped part of it off. That sounds minor, but without a fender the wheels threw moon dust all over everything. So the crew (with help from Mission Control) taped four lunar maps together and stuck them on with duct tape. Good old American gray tape, basically. It worked.

Before he climbed back up the ladder for the last time, Cernan wrote "TDC" in the dust — his daughter Tracy's initials. Those letters are probably still there, since there's no wind on the Moon to wipe them away. The buggy, the descent stage, all of it just sits in that valley, waiting.
