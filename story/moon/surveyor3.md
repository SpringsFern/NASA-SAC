## the robot that got a second visitor

Surveyor 3 landed in 1967 to prove a soft landing was even possible. It did its job, dug some trenches, took over 6,000 pictures, and then the two-week-long lunar night came and it never woke up again. Cold killed it.

It just sat there, dead, for about two and a half years. Then in 1969 the Apollo 12 crew landed nearby and literally walked over to it. Pete Conrad and Alan Bean checked it out, took photos, and unbolted some parts to bring home, including the camera. Scientists wanted to see what years on the Moon did to the hardware.

That camera is now in the Smithsonian. The rest of Surveyor 3 is still in its crater, a little lighter than it used to be. It's basically the only probe that got a repair-visit... except they took parts instead of fixing it.
