## the science station that outlived the mission

Apollo 12 was the second landing, and it was way more precise than Apollo 11. They landed close enough to walk over to an old robot probe (more on that in the Surveyor 3 pin).

The big thing they left was ALSEP, a little nuclear-powered science station. It had a seismometer, a magnetometer, and a few other instruments, all hooked to a central box that sent data back to Earth. The astronauts set it up and then just... left it running. And it kept going for years. NASA finally turned it off in 1977, mostly because of budget cuts, not because it broke.

Fun/sad detail: they brought the first color TV camera to the Moon, but it died early because someone pointed it at the Sun and burned the sensor. Oops.

Oh and when they crashed the empty ascent stage into the Moon on purpose, the seismometer picked it up and the vibrations kept going for almost an hour. One scientist said the Moon "rang like a bell."
