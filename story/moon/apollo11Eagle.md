## the launchpad that never left

So the lunar module had two parts. The top part (ascent stage) flew the crew back up to orbit. The bottom part (descent stage) stayed on the ground and basically became a launchpad. That bottom half is still right here, with the plaque and the flag nearby.

Before they left, Armstrong and Aldrin tossed out a bunch of stuff they didn't need anymore. Boots, tools, cameras, empty food bags, the backpacks that kept them alive outside. Roughly 100 items total. It sounds messy but it was on purpose, every kilo they dropped meant more moon rocks they could bring home.

The cool part? The laser reflector they set down is still used today. Scientists bounce lasers off it to measure the distance to the Moon. So this "abandoned" site is honestly still doing science.
